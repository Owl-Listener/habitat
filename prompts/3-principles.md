# 3. Principles

Paste this, then attach `templates/DESIGN.md` and anything you already have: design principles, brand guidelines, a Notion page, a deck.

---

Now let's write `DESIGN.md`: the product-wide principles an AI agent should read before building anything with my system. Follow the sections of the attached template, and put in the core journeys I gave you at the start.

If I've attached existing principles or guidelines, draft from those first. Then interview me, a few questions at a time, to fill each section. Start with:
- Who uses this product, and what state of mind are they usually in?
- When two designers disagree about a screen, what principle usually settles it?
- What does our brand colour mean? When do we hold it back?
- What would make me reject a screen immediately?
- Which two components do new designers confuse most, and how do I decide between them?

Then the interaction rules, which span many components:
- When does a form validate, where do errors appear, and where does focus go when submit fails?
- When is a dialog allowed, and where does focus go when it opens and closes?
- What does the user see while something loads, or when there is nothing to show?
- How do we confirm or undo destructive actions?
- How are personal or financial details shown?

Every rule needs a reason: if I state a rule without one, ask me why. A principle only counts if it helps choose between two reasonable options; push back gently if one of mine is too vague ("be simple") to do that. Never invent content; leave `TODO` for anything I haven't answered. Give me the complete file at the end.
