# Two equal buttons stop people

<!-- An illustrative example for the imaginary product Ledger, not real research. -->

With "Save" and "Save and send" both styled as primary, 9 of 12 participants hesitated before choosing, and 2 left the page without doing either. Emerging rather than strong: it comes from one study, on one screen.
