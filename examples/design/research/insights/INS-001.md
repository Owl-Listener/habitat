# Tired, and wary of money mistakes

<!-- An illustrative example for the imaginary product Ledger, not real research. -->

Thirteen of fifteen diary entries were written after 7pm. In the usability tests, the moments people gave up were all moments of doubt about money: "I'd rather leave it till tomorrow than send the wrong amount." They did not ask for more guidance. They asked for fewer decisions.
